03 · THE METHOD & REAL NASA DATA

We never say '87% Mars': we separate Terrain (X), Climate (Y), and Limits (B)

1. Terrain We CAN Test (X)

30 m slope (°), local relief (m), roughness, albedo, solar/UV flux, hyper-aridity (10 variables).

2. Climate Response (Y)

Day–night temperature swing ΔT (K), cooling rate, wind speed, dust (6 variables).

3. What Earth CANNOT Imitate (B)

Gravity (0.38 g / 0.166 g), thin CO2 air (~0.6 kPa) or vacuum (0 kPa), cosmic radiation (~230–380 mSv/yr).

30 m Comparison: Wasserstein-1 (W1 in ° & m) + Kolmogorov–Smirnov (D_KS) + 2D Block Bootstrap (95% CI)
