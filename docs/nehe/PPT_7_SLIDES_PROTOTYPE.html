Slide 2 of 7 · Real Problem, Human Importance, Value Proposition & Innovation Thesis

"We Do Not Seek to Prove That Atacama Is Identical to Mars"

Our Innovation Proposal: "We do not seek to prove that Atacama is identical to Mars. We seek to show which characteristics it shares, which differences exist, and which new locations deserve to be studied using scientific evidence."

1. Is the Problem Real?

Yes. NASA recognizes that terrestrial analogs are not yet fully characterized and that real opportunities exist to identify new sites for Moon and Mars exploration by integrating multi-instrument scientific data and understanding each terrain's physical limitations.

2. Does It Affect People?

Yes. It directly affects researchers and engineers who need transparent preliminary evidence to prepare field trials, while opening an educational opportunity to bring space science to students and citizens without scientific backgrounds through accessible tools.

3. Value Proposition

Ckepi facilitates transparent scientific comparisons, guides preliminary research (pre-selecting sites rather than certifying operational safety), enables accessible learning with real space data, and studies under-characterized sectors of Antofagasta and other regions.

One Causal Engine, Two Synchronized User Experiences

Built with React 18, TypeScript (0 any), Tailwind CSS, MapLibre GL v6, FastAPI, and DuckDB—responsive from 320px mobile screens to 4K mission walls.

Mode A · Mission Telemetry For Scientists & Engineers

  • Dual 2D/3D Planetary Canvas: Synchronized Earth H3 grid + NASA Moon Trek & Mars Trek WMTS 3D Globes.
  • 5 Mission Presets + Live Coordinate Probe: Click anywhere on Earth to query real-time ETL (Copernicus DEM, NASA POWER, Open-Meteo, SoilGrids, Macrostrat).
  • Real 30 m Terrain Distribution Panel: Interactive histograms, empirical CDFs, W₁ & D_KS metrics, 95% block-bootstrap CIs, 30/60/90 m sensitivity, and 1-click export (.md / .csv / .geojson).

Mode B · Explorer ("Three Worlds in Your Hand") For All Ages

  • Verified NASA NSSDCA Table: Compares Earth, Moon, and Mars in 3 progressive levels (Look → Compare → Investigate) using everyday analogies ("A 10 kg backpack feels like 1.7 kg on the Moon and 3.8 kg on Mars").
  • Web Speech API Voice Narration: Full spoken readout in English, Spanish, and Portuguese for low digital literacy, classrooms, and older adults.
  • Mobile-First Ergonomics: Touch targets ≥ 44×44 px, WCAG AA/AAA contrast, printable cards, and 15-second STEAM challenges.

Slide 6 of 7 · Irreducible Physical Gap (B) & Local Impact (Caja La Araucana)

Where Earth Analogs Always Break Down & Territorial Value in Antofagasta

Irreducible Gap (B) Why Earth Never Matches

  • Gravity (g): Mars 0.38 g (3.7 m/s²) & Moon 0.166 g (1.6 m/s²) vs. Earth 1.00 g (9.8 m/s²) → Over-stresses rover suspension and alters regolith sinkage.
  • Atmosphere (P_atm): Mars ~0.6 kPa (6.1 mbar, 95% CO₂) & Moon 0 kPa (vacuum) vs. Earth 101.3 kPa → Invalidates open-air thermal convection and rotor lift.
  • Ionizing Radiation: Mars ~230 mSv/yr (Curiosity/RAD, 0.64 mSv/day) & Moon ~380 mSv/yr vs. Earth ~2.4 mSv/yr → Protected by Earth's magnetosphere + atmosphere.
  • Thermal Swing (ΔT): Moon ~290 K (95–390 K) & Mars mean −59 to −65 °C vs. Earth mean 15 °C.
