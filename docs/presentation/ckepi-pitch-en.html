Causal Decomposition (X → Y) & Breakage Boundaries (B)

10 Independent Forcings (X)

Roughness, slope, thermal inertia, albedo, solar flux, subsurface ice, aridity, RH, UV index, LAI.

6 Dependent Responses (Y)

Diurnal ΔT, nocturnal cooling, mean surface temp, wind speed, aeolian dust flux, ice sublimation.

3 Breakage Boundaries (B)

Surface gravity (g), atmospheric pressure (mbar), and ionizing cosmic radiation (mSv/yr).
