CKEPI: Multi-Planetary Analog Discovery Engine with Causal Decomposition (X → Y ∥ B)

No place on Earth is 100% identical to the Moon or Mars. Ckepi replaces black-box similarity scores with explicit causal subspaces over a global Uber H3 hexagonal grid and a 30 m common-scale statistical terrain engine.

Subspace X Independent Substrate

Slope (°), Roughness (m), Surface Albedo, Thermal Inertia (J m⁻²K⁻¹s⁻¹/²), Solar Irradiance (W/m²), and Aridity Index.

Subspace Y = f(X) Environmental Response

Diurnal Temperature Amplitude ΔT (K), Nocturnal Cooling Rate (K/h), and Aeolian Dust Suspension Risk.

Subspace B Irreducible Physical Gap

Gravity (0.38 g Mars / 0.166 g Moon), Pressure (0.6 kPa CO₂ / 0 kPa), and Ionizing Radiation (~230–380 mSv/yr) quantified via R_break.

Slide 2 of 7 · Problem Statement & Status Quo Failure

Why "Atacama Looks Like Mars" Is Scientifically Insufficient

Selecting terrestrial testbeds by visual resemblance or regional reputation introduces three severe scientific and engineering failures:

Failure 1 Scale & Resolution Blindness

Comparing kilometric global orbital grids directly against meter-scale rover testbeds, or summarizing an entire crater into a single mean slope without confidence intervals.

Failure 2 Confusing Biology with Mechanics

Using flat hyperarid plains (like classic Yungay) to test rover traction intended for steep crater-margin scarps (like Jezero Crater), resulting in false mechanical confidence.

Failure 3 Ignoring Physical Rupture (B)

Testing hardware in open air on Earth (1.0 g, 101.3 kPa, 2.4 mSv/yr) without flagging which subsystems will fail under 0.38 g, 0.6 kPa CO₂, or 230 mSv/yr GCR radiation.

Slide 6 of 7 · Irreducible Physical Gap (B) & Local Impact (Caja La Araucana)

Where Earth Analogs Always Break Down & Territorial Value in Antofagasta

Irreducible Gap (B) Why Earth Never Matches

  • Gravity (g): Mars 0.38 g (3.72 m/s²) & Moon 0.166 g (1.62 m/s²) vs. Earth 1.00 g (9.81 m/s²) → Over-stresses rover suspension and alters regolith sinkage.
  • Atmosphere (P_atm): Mars ~0.6 kPa (6.1 mbar CO₂) & Moon 0 kPa (vacuum) vs. Earth 101.3 kPa → Invalidates open-air thermal convection and rotor lift.
  • Ionizing Radiation: Mars ~230 mSv/yr (Curiosity/RAD) & Moon ~380 mSv/yr vs. Earth ~2.4 mSv/yr → Requires particle accelerators for avionics SEU testing.
  • Thermal Swing (ΔT): Moon ~290 K & Mars 80–100 K vs. Atacama 35–40 K.

Caja La Araucana & Official UN SDGs 4 Strategic Axes

  • 1. Frontier Science for All (SDG 4.3): Free, voice-guided (Web Speech API), trilingual (EN/ES/PT) platform accessible to any school, technical high school, or family without digital barriers.

  • 3. Local Application (SDG 9.5 · SDG 10 · SDG 13): Flags under-studied Antofagasta locations (e.g. H3 cell 83b224, the closest slope match to Jezero) as candidates for field study — pre-selection, not validation.
